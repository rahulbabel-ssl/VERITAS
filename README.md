# VERITAS

**An AI agent that remembers what didn't work, so labs stop repeating the same failures.**

Scientific and engineering records are biased toward success: failed experiments, dead-end routes, and hard-won tacit knowledge ("this regulator oscillates with that capacitor", "never use that supplier's part") stay buried in notebooks or leave with the people who learned them. As a result, teams keep re-attempting things a colleague already knows fail. Veritas ingests messy lab notes, turns them into structured records of what was tried, under what conditions, and why it failed, and lets you ask "have we tried this before?" with answers that cite their original source. Initial focus: electronics and hardware labs.

**Work in progress.** Veritas is under active development. Expect breaking changes, and check back for updates.
